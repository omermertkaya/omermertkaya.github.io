**Ömer Mert Kaya is an Identity & Access Management Engineer specializing in IAM, IGA, IDM, PAM, authentication and authorization.**

Welcome to my personal blog. I'm Ömer Mert Kaya.

## Primary and High School

I was born on the night of March 1st, 1995, in Ankara. I completed my primary and secondary education in Akçakoca and Düzce. In the early 2000s, as home computers became widespread in Turkey, I got my own first computer in 2005. Around the same time I discovered the internet and started building what you could call Web 1.0 content sites.

I started out using Mynet's "mysite" website builder, picking ready-made themes to put a site together quickly. Because I always wanted to know what was going on underneath the hood, and wanted my site to look better, I eventually started building pages with HTML and CSS that I taught myself along the way. There wasn't much content about this on the Turkish internet at the time, so it took a while before I realized these were actually the foundations of the World Wide Web.

In 2009 I got into Ankara Tuzluçayır Anadolu Lisesi (Anatolian High School). Alongside school, I bought my first domain and hosting and launched my first WordPress ventures. AdSense and a local Turkish ad network called Adkur brought in my first money from the web.

## University

### Computer Engineering and LC Waikiki

In 2014 I was admitted to the Computer Engineering department at Süleyman Demirel University. Since the state student loan (KYK) wasn't enough to live on, I had to support myself. When what I earned from the web wasn't enough either, I took my first real job as a sales associate at LC Waikiki. If you've ever been to İyaşpark Mall in Isparta, you might have run into me there between 2015 and 2019.

## Internships

Balancing school and work while also landing an internship was genuinely difficult, but I pulled it off.

I interned at Ataseven Teknoloji in April–May 2019, where I built a Flappy Bird–style game in C# with the Unity engine and published it on the Google Play Store.

In May–June 2019 I interned at Data Kontrol Bilişim Teknolojileri, contributing to CRM products built with C# on the .NET framework.

## Military Service and First Job

### Ministry of National Defense, Sivas

After five years of juggling university and work, I graduated as a computer engineer somewhere in the middle of my class. I started applying for jobs right away, but mandatory military service stood in the way. I gave up my deferment, and in January 2020 I was excited to be assigned as a reserve officer to the Ministry of National Defense's Sivas Construction and Real Estate Regional Directorate.

## Work Experience

### Türk Telekom

Knowing I'd need a job before my service ended, I started looking around late 2020. Over 4–5 months I went through interviews for a Junior Broadcast System Engineer role at Türk Telekom. Since hiring at large corporations follows a defined roadmap and takes time, I spent January through May sharpening my skills in JavaScript, Node.js, and Django web frameworks. Since I'd be working as a systems engineer, I also reinforced my knowledge of operating systems, virtual servers, and core networking. I joined Türk Telekom in May 2021.

### AgeSA

In mid-2022, with rising inflation pushing me to turn a new page in my career, I received an offer from AgeSA Life and Pension for an Identity and Access Management Associate Specialist role, and I took it, leaving a Türk Telekom job I had genuinely loved.

My time at Türk Telekom was an extremely valuable stage of my professional growth; the knowledge and skills I gained there remain one of the cornerstones of my career.

I started my career at AgeSA as an Associate Specialist and, over three years and two promotions, rose to Senior Specialist.

It was a productive period in which I both deepened my technical expertise and took on significant responsibilities at the organizational level.

### Monofor

At this stage of my career, I had the opportunity to join Monofor, a US-headquartered Identity Security platform delivering end-to-end IDM, IAM, IGA, and PAM solutions. Within the vendor organization, I contribute directly to product development while also, in a consulting capacity, successfully bringing this technology to some of Turkey's leading institutions.

Today at Monofor, I work on end-to-end Identity Security solutions spanning identity lifecycle management, identity governance and administration (IGA), privileged access management (PAM), and authentication/authorization processes.
