User lifecycle (User Lifecycle Management) covers the cycle a user or employee goes through, starting from the moment they are first registered in the system until the day they leave it.

We can better understand the process by looking at the steps in this cycle. To make these steps a bit clearer, I'll continue the explanation using the process we've designed for our own employees.

### 1) User Registration (Provisioning)

- Registering the new employee in the system and creating their accounts
- Assigning entitlements (permissions and roles)
