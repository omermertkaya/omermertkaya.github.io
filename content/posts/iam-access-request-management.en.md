Access request management refers to the process by which employees, or business partners who have access to our systems, request permissions in the applications we manage with IAM. We can manage this process through the self-service screens offered by IAM products. This way, users can request the permissions they need directly through the system. Alternatively, companies use request management systems such as Service Manager (SM); integrating these systems with IAM allows requests to be managed centrally.

### Advantages of Managing Access Requests with IAM

IAM-based access request management automatically grants the permission or role requests users need in applications, but only after the required approvals. The advantages this approach provides include:

Speed and Security Through Automation: User requests are automatically processed after the approval steps, and the required permissions are defined. This eliminates the need for manual intervention by the admins who own the application.

Centralized Management: All permissions and roles managed through the IAM system are tracked within a central structure. This makes it easier to see and manage which permissions and roles users hold.

As a result, automating IAM processes reduces operational overhead, speeds up the process, and increases the level of security.

### What Are the Steps in Access Request Management?

Access request management takes place within a defined flow:

- Creating the Request: The user requests the access permission they need through the IAM system's self-service screen or a request management system.

- Reviewing the Request: This request is evaluated and reviewed according to authorization policies.

- Approval or Rejection: The request is either approved or rejected by the designated authorized person or system.

- Granting Access: If the request is approved, the required access permission is defined for the user.

### The Contribution of Access Request Management to Business Processes

Access request management plays a critical role in ensuring security and efficiency in business processes. Supported by automation, this process eliminates manual operations, reducing security vulnerabilities, strengthening compliance, and enabling a more effective use of the workforce. Organizations can optimize their business processes and build a more efficient workflow by managing access requests through IAM solutions.

In conclusion, access request processes managed with IAM increase businesses' digital security and efficiency while greatly simplifying their business processes. With the right IAM strategy, organizations can move forward into the future on more solid ground by providing a secure working environment.
